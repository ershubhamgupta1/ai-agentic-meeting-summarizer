## Speaker Enrollment Sample Scripts

This section contains **recommended sample speeches** to record your voice for **speaker enrollment**.  
Using these scripts helps the system learn your voice accurately and improves identification confidence.

### Recording Guidelines
- Record in a **quiet environment**
- Use the **same microphone** for all recordings
- Speak **naturally** (not loudly, not slowly)
- Each recording should be **10–15 seconds**
- Save recordings in **WAV format (16kHz, mono)**

---

### 🎤 Sample Speech 1 – Neutral (Best for Baseline)

Use this for a clean, neutral voice sample.

> Hello, this is a sample recording of my voice.  
> I am speaking at a natural pace so the system can learn my voice clearly.  
> This recording is only for speaker identification and testing purposes.

---

### 🎤 Sample Speech 2 – Conversational Tone

Use this to capture your everyday speaking style.

> Hi there, I’m recording my voice to help the system recognize me later.  
> I’m speaking normally, just like I would in a regular conversation.  
> This should give a clear and consistent voice sample.

---

### 🎤 Sample Speech 3 – Slightly Expressive (Controlled)

Use this to add mild variation while staying clear and calm.

> Today I’m creating a voice sample so my system can identify me correctly.  
> I’m speaking clearly, calmly, and without any background noise.  
> This recording will help improve accuracy.

---

### 🎤 Sample Speech 4 – Longer (Recommended for Enrollment)

**This is the most important script.**  
Use this as your primary enrollment recording.

> This is a longer voice sample for speaker enrollment.  
> I am speaking continuously for a few seconds so the system can capture my vocal characteristics.  
> My tone, pitch, and speaking style are natural and relaxed.

---

### 🎤 Sample Speech 5 – Natural Everyday Speech

Use this to capture how you sound in meetings or calls.

> I’m just talking normally, the way I would during a meeting or a phone call.  
> There is nothing special about this recording except that it represents my real voice.  
> This should help the system recognize me accurately.

---

### Recommended Usage
- Record **Sample Speech 4** (mandatory)
- Record **any one** additional sample
- Two high-quality recordings are better than many short or rushed ones

These recordings are used for **speaker embedding and identification** and directly affect recognition accuracy.
